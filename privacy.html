Privacy policy

Last updated 11 November 2026

This policy explains how Beauty Edition Lash Atelier (“we”, “us”) collects, uses and protects your personal information when you use this website, contact us or book a treatment, in line with the Protection of Personal Information Act 4 of 2013 (POPIA).

What we collect

This website has no accounts, no online payments and no visitor database. We only receive personal information when you choose to contact us by WhatsApp, phone or social media. That can include your name, phone number, the treatment and date you ask about, and anything else you write in your message.

The booking form on this website does not store or send anything by itself. It prepares a message in WhatsApp on your own device, and nothing reaches us unless you press send there.

When you come in for a treatment we may ask about allergies, sensitivities, skin conditions or medication that could affect your lash or skin treatment. This is health information, and we only collect it with your consent and use it to keep you safe.

Why we use it

  • To answer your questions and arrange, confirm or move your appointments.
  • To record your deposit and payments.
  • To choose products and techniques that are safe and suitable for you.
  • To keep the business records we are required to keep by law.

We will not send you marketing messages unless you ask us to, and you can ask us to stop at any time.

Photographs

We only take or share before and after photos of you with your permission. You can withdraw that permission at any time and we will remove the photos from our website and social media.

Cookies and tracking

This website does not use analytics, advertising trackers or cookies. Our fonts and images are served from this website itself.

Other services

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may keep standard technical logs such as IP addresses to keep the service secure. Links to WhatsApp, Instagram, TikTok and Facebook take you to those services, and their own privacy policies apply once you are there.

Sharing your information

We do not sell or rent your personal information. We only share it when the law requires us to, or when you have asked us to.

How long we keep it

We keep client notes for as long as you are a client and for a reasonable time afterwards, and financial records for as long as the law requires. After that we delete them securely.

Your rights

Under POPIA you may ask whether we hold personal information about you, ask for a copy, ask us to correct or delete it, and object to how we use it. To do so, contact us on 083 303 6393.

If you are unhappy with how we handled your information, you may complain to the Information Regulator of South Africa at inforegulator.org.za.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows when it last changed.
